Inside the app

What Pulse shows.

Car
Current vehicle state plus commands that distinguish accepted requests from confirmed changes.
Drives and charging
Sessions, energy, cost, efficiency, battery behavior, and weekly totals.
Shared ETA
A private, time-limited trip link that opens in any browser. No app or sign-in is required for the recipient.
