Ludic Hub · In development

Keep TeslaCam footage local.

Hub is optional hardware in development for archiving TeslaCam footage, finding clips by time and camera, and transferring selected footage to an iPhone near the car.

What Hub adds

The planned workflow.

Archive
Keep supported TeslaCam clips in a local library near the vehicle.
Browse
Find recordings by time and camera instead of searching raw folders.
Transfer
Use Bluetooth discovery and private Wi-Fi to move a selected clip nearby while the iPhone keeps cellular service.

Development status

No price or release date yet.

Hub is active development hardware. Its enclosure, specifications, price, and availability have not been announced and may change. Pulse remains usable without Hub.
