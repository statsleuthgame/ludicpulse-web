Ludic Hub · In development

Your TeslaCam, finally organized.

Hub is an optional local hardware layer for Ludic Pulse. It is being built to archive TeslaCam footage, help you find the moment that matters, and move selected clips to your iPhone when you are near the car.

Why it exists

The car records. Hub makes the recording usable.

TeslaCam is valuable at exactly the moment you need evidence, context, or a memory from the road. Hub is being designed to reduce the friction between raw files in the car and the clip you want on your phone.

01

Archive locally

Collect supported TeslaCam footage into a persistent library so recent clips are not your only view of what the car recorded.

02

Find the moment

Browse and filter recordings by time and camera instead of digging through removable storage one file at a time.

03

Bring it to iPhone

When you are nearby, Pulse can connect over Bluetooth and the Hub's private Wi-Fi to preview, download, keep, and share selected files.

04

Add drive context

When matching vehicle data is available, Hub can help Pulse connect footage with richer local route and telemetry context.

Local by design

Near the car. Under your control.

Hub is designed around a direct, nearby phone connection. Your iPhone can stay on cellular while it talks to the Hub's private Wi-Fi, and only the footage you choose needs to leave the car.

Development status

Clear about what is real—and what comes next.

Hub is active development hardware, not a finished retail promise. This is the capability boundary we are building against today.

Current direction

  • Optional companion to Ludic Pulse
  • Local TeslaCam archive and footage library
  • Nearby iPhone discovery and transfer
  • Route and telemetry enrichment when available

Not announced

  • Final industrial design or enclosure
  • Retail price or release date
  • Final storage and performance specifications
  • A guarantee of gap-free recording during every storage transition
