Ludic Hub · In development

Your TeslaCam, finally organized.

Hub is optional Ludic Pulse hardware for archiving TeslaCam footage locally, finding clips quickly, and moving selected footage to your iPhone near the car. Your iPhone can stay on cellular while it connects directly to Hub's private Wi-Fi.

Get Hub updatesSee Pulse

What Hub adds

From raw files to the clip you need.

01

Archive

Keep supported TeslaCam footage in a local library so recent clips are not your only view.

02

Find

Browse recordings by time and camera, then surface the moment that matters.

03

Download

Use Bluetooth discovery and private Wi-Fi to move selected clips nearby for preview, saving, or sharing.

Development status

Built locally. Still taking shape.

Hub is active development hardware. Its industrial design, specifications, price, and availability are not yet announced and may change. Only selected footage needs to leave the car, and Pulse remains usable without Hub.
