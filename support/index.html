Support

Setup, troubleshooting, and account help for the current Ludic Pulse beta.

Set up Pulse

  1. Sign in with Apple. This creates your Ludic account.
  2. Connect Tesla. Authorize through Tesla and choose a vehicle.
  3. Finish tracking setup. Keep the vehicle awake while Pulse completes the steps shown in Settings.

Common fixes

  • If automatic tracking is not ready, wake the vehicle, open Car → Settings → Tesla connected, and retry the indicated setup stage.
  • If data is stale, use the Car refresh button once. Pulse avoids constant Tesla polling while telemetry is healthy.
  • If a command is accepted but not confirmed, check the Tesla app or the vehicle before repeating it.
  • Shared ETA appears only while Tesla navigation has a fresh destination. A link ends at arrival, route cancellation or replacement, manual stop, or four hours.
  • To preview the interface before connecting a Tesla, choose Explore demo on the Car tab.

Contact support

Email support

Include the app build, screen name, approximate time, and what you expected. Never send a password, access token, full VIN, precise location, or unredacted screenshot containing private data.

Account and Tesla access

Data export, device-session revocation, and permanent account deletion are available in Settings. Tesla authorization and vehicle virtual keys are managed separately.
