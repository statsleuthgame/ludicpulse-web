Ludic Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026 · United States beta

The short version

You can create a Ludic account with Apple and separately authorize Tesla vehicle access. We do not receive your Apple or Tesla password, sell personal data, run advertising, or use data to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Exact route history is optional.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Ludic Technologies LLC provides Ludic Pulse and handles data in the iOS app, cloud service, Live Activities, and optional hardware. Ludic Hub provides dashcam footage; the Orbiter knob remains compatible with existing development hardware. The service is currently offered to US beta users and is not directed to children under 13.

Location, routes, and Shared ETA

Vehicle location may be used transiently to show the latest parked location and provide directions. Exact route points are excluded from route history unless you turn on Save route history. Shared ETA is separate, manual, and off by default: when you create a link, the latest position, destination when permitted, Tesla ETA and distance, and route geometry when available are shown to anyone with that link until arrival, route cancellation or replacement, manual stop, or four hours. If Tesla does not provide route geometry, the recipient browser may ask Apple Maps for an estimated remaining route between the current position and destination; it is labeled as estimated, while Tesla remains the source of ETA and remaining distance. The plaintext token is kept in the URL fragment and iOS Keychain, not the database. Public views have no analytics, ads, replay, or third-party tracking. Screenshots, session replay, VINs, coordinates, tokens, request bodies, email, and user identity are excluded from Sentry diagnostics.

Service providers and disclosures

  • Tesla supplies authorized vehicle data and receives commands that you request.
  • Microsoft Azure hosts the Ludic Pulse API, database, cache, and encrypted backups.
  • Resend delivers an operator email containing the name, email address, and submission time when someone requests private beta access.
  • Sentry receives bounded, sanitized crash and performance diagnostics.
  • Apple authenticates users who choose Sign in with Apple, distributes the app, processes APNs/Live Activity tokens and notifications, presents user-initiated share sheets, renders Shared ETA maps, and may calculate clearly labeled estimated route geometry when Tesla omits it.

Providers may process data only for the services they supply to Ludic Pulse. We may also disclose data when legally required or to protect users and the service. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Security

Network traffic is encrypted in transit. App credentials are stored as hashes by the service and in iOS Keychain on the device. Apple and Tesla refresh credentials are encrypted at rest. Apple identity tokens are verified for issuer, audience, signature, and one-time nonce. Tesla OAuth uses a short-lived, single-use PKCE exchange. Provider-link challenges are random, short-lived, and single-use. Access is scoped to the signed-in user and selected vehicle. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure; report suspected issues to support.

Your choices and rights

Settings provides route-history controls, individual opt-in notifications, Live Activity privacy controls, structured data export, device-session revocation, and permanent in-app account deletion. Deletion first revokes stored Sign in with Apple authorization, then removes the user's credentials, linked identities, preferences, sessions, attributable usage, and user-associated data; it also attempts to stop telemetry when no other legitimate Ludic Pulse user shares the vehicle. Tesla consent and a vehicle virtual key remain controlled separately, so you may also revoke third-party access in Tesla and remove Ludic Pulse's virtual key from the vehicle.
